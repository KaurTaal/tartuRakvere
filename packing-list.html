📋 Notes to remember
🦟
Bug spray + tick checks — critical
Late May is peak tick season in Estonian forests. Apply bug spray daily and do a full body tick check every evening — groin, armpits, back of knees, hairline. A tick removal card is tiny and worth packing.
💧
Water — confirm you have bottles!
Neither list explicitly mentions water bottles. You need at least 1L capacity each. If using natural water sources on trail, bring purification tablets or a filter. Don't assume all checkpoints have water.
🌙
Hammock without a mat — watch the cold
No mat needed in a hammock — you're suspended. But your sleeping bag's insulation gets compressed under you and loses warmth. If the night drops below ~10°C, stuff a fleece or jacket underneath your back and legs. Estonian May nights can surprise you.
🦶
Blisters are the #1 issue on long hikes
150km over 5 days is ~30km/day. Pack more blister plasters than you think you need. Treat hot spots immediately — don't wait until a blister forms. Wool socks reduce friction significantly.
🔋
2 charging cables — phone + watch
Easy to forget one of them. Pack both in your electronics dry bag. Also confirm your power bank has enough capacity for 5 days with limited charging access.
🗺️
Download offline maps before you leave
Mobile data may be patchy on the trail. Download the route offline on Maps.me, OsmAnd, or save the Google My Maps to your device. Know the rough checkpoints and distances in advance.
⛺
Confirm tarp from partner before departure
Your partner is buying a tarp for you — make sure it arrives and you test the setup before day 1. A tarp rigged wrong in the rain is miserable. Practice once at home.
